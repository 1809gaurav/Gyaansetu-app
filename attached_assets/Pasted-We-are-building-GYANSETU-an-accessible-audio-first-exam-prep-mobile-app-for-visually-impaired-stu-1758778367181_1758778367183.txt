We are building GYANSETU — an accessible, audio-first exam-prep mobile app for visually impaired students that should look and feel like an official government app. Use React Native or Flutter (mobile-first, web later). Follow these requirements exactly.

Problem Statement Recap

Visually impaired students face huge barriers because most prep apps are text-heavy and not optimized for screen readers. They need an audio-first, accessible app that delivers questions, explanations, and progress tracking through voice and sound-based interactions.

Brand & Visual Direction (Government look & feel)

App name: GYANSETU (use everywhere as the product name).

Make the UI feel official and trustworthy like a government app:

Use dignified, uncluttered layout, clear sections, and minimal ornamentation.

Primary color palette: saffron (#FF9933) — white — India green (#138808) (use these subtly and accessibly).

Include an Indian-flag-inspired element in the header or splash screen (e.g., a narrow saffron-white-green ribbon or a stylized tricolor bar). Ensure the flag element is respectful, non-intrusive, and accessible (high contrast).

Use a simple emblem-like logo for GYANSETU that hints at the tricolor—avoid replicating the Ashoka Chakra in exact form if unsure of legal constraints; prefer a stylized motif using the three colors or a simple book + wave motif in saffron/green/white.

Typography: highly legible, large sizes. UI must comply with accessibility contrast ratios.

Tone: formal, supportive, and official-sounding microcopy (e.g., “GYANSETU — Official Accessible Exam Prep”).

Proposed Solution (features recap)

Audio-first interaction: All content read aloud via TTS.

Voice navigation: Full controls by speech commands.

Accessible UI: Large fonts, high contrast, TalkBack/VoiceOver support.

Adaptive learning: Tracks weak areas; personalized audio revisions.

Offline support: Downloadable question packs.

Key Features

Audio Quiz Mode — TTS reads questions + options; answer by voice or keypad; immediate spoken feedback.

Voice Navigation & Commands — e.g., “Next question”, “Repeat”, “Explain answer”, “Skip”, “Mark for review”.

Accessible Design — Dark/light modes, high-contrast toggle (with tricolor theme option), large tap targets, haptic confirmation.

Progress Tracking — Spoken summaries; exportable reports (future Braille export).

Content Packs — Competitive exam packs, downloadable for offline use.

Technical Approach

Frontend (Mobile App)

Framework: React Native or Flutter. Cross-platform for Android & iOS.

Accessibility: native TalkBack/VoiceOver, semantic accessibility labels, large fonts, adjustable text size.

Audio: Use device TTS for offline; optional cloud TTS (Azure/Google) for natural voices.

Branding: Splash screen and header that incorporate the Indian tricolor ribbon and the GYANSETU emblem. Ensure icons and graphical elements have accessible alt text.

Backend

DB: Firebase Firestore / Supabase.

API: REST or GraphQL for fetching questions, tracking progress, managing downloads.

Content Delivery: Pre-loaded packs, secure downloadable bundles.

Voice Recognition

Primary: Google Speech-to-Text (multilingual: Hindi/English).

Offline fallback: simple keyword/DTMF recognition for A/B/C/D or numeric answers.

AI Layer (Optional Advanced)

Adaptive ML to recommend practice areas.

Auto-summarization to convert explanations into short, spoken sentences.

Architecture Flow

App opens → GYANSETU splash (tricolor ribbon + emblem) → audio-guided login.

Select language (Hindi / English).

Choose subject/exam pack (voice or touch).

App reads Q + options aloud.

Answer via voice or keypad.

System validates + responds with spoken feedback.

Progress saved to backend.

Weekly audio report generated.

UI/UX Implementation Notes (government style + accessibility)

Splash screen: GYANSETU name, small tricolor ribbon across top or bottom, short audio welcome: “Welcome to GYANSETU — Official Accessible Exam Prep.”

Home screen: large tiles with clear labels, an always-available “Voice Command” button, and a visible “Download Packs” section.

Color/tone: default light theme uses white background with saffron-green accents; provide a high-contrast tricolor-friendly theme toggle.

Iconography: simple, easily-discernible icons; label every control with accessibility labels.

Onboarding: audio-first tutorial that demonstrates voice commands and navigation.

Deliverables — what to generate now

Project scaffold (React Native or Flutter) with folder structure.

Sample splash screen + home screen UI implementing tricolor and GYANSETU logo placeholder.

Example TTS integration (device TTS) and Speech-to-Text stub (Google STT).

Simple offline question pack downloader + local storage example.

Accessibility checklist & sample ARIA/label usage for key screens.

README with how to run, brand guidelines (tricolor usage rules), and assets placeholders.

Extra constraints & guidance

Respectful and modest use of the Indian flag colors—do not misuse the national flag. Use a ribbon or motif rather than a literal flag in UI elements where legal/ethical issues could arise.

All UI elements must meet WCAG AA contrast by default.

Keep copy formal and official-sounding for trustworthiness.

Prioritize offline-first experience and low-bandwidth operation.
